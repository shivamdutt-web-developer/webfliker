Last Updated: November 2026 • Global Commercial Retainer Framework

1. Professional Scope & Retainers

Webflikr provides enterprise organic search optimization, generative engine optimization (GEO), 3D web design, and digital marketing retainerships. Services commence upon mutual execution of a digital statement of work (SOW) or retainer agreement.

2. Month-to-Month Flexibility

Consistent with our enterprise search heritage, our standard search retainerships operate on a transparent month-to-month basis unless a multi-quarter enterprise agreement is explicitly contracted. Clients may modify or pause retainers with standard 30-day written notice.

3. Intellectual Property Ownership

All custom technical architectures, schema JSON-LD scripts, keyword intelligence graphs, custom 3D WebGL assets, and published content produced under active retainership belong 100% to the client upon receipt of contract billing.

4. Search Engine Compliance & Guarantees

We practice strictly 100% White-Hat, Google Quality Evaluator and algorithmic-compliant search engineering. While organic rankings cannot be guaranteed by any third party due to search engine volatility, we adhere strictly to verified KPI benchmarks outlined in monthly deliverables matrices.

5. Governing Law & Arbitration

Commercial contracts are governed by standard commercial agency guidelines under the jurisdiction of New Delhi (India), London (UK), or Chicago (USA) corresponding to the client's contracting entity.
