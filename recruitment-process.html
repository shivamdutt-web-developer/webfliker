STAGE-BY-STAGE JOURNEY

The 5 Steps from Candidate to Team Member

A clear roadmap so you always know what to expect next.

01

Portfolio & Resume Review (48 Hours)

Our directors personally review your past work, GitHub code repositories, live URLs, or verified search ranking case studies. We value tangible outputs over resume buzzwords.

02

Introductory Cultural Conversation (30 Mins)

A casual 1-on-1 video call with our talent team. We discuss your background, ideal working environment, learning ambitions, and compensation expectations to ensure mutual alignment.

03

Practical Technical Assessment (Take-Home)

A targeted mini challenge designed to take 2–3 hours maximum. For SEOs, it might be diagnosing a crawl log anomaly; for developers, optimizing a Three.js shader or Next.js page.

04

Technical Deep-Dive with Squad Leads (45 Mins)

Walk through your take-home solution with the senior architects you'll be collaborating with. We discuss your thought process, trade-offs, and alternative approaches.

05

Offer Presentation & Welcome Kit

We present a competitive, transparent written offer letter including base compensation, performance bonuses, health coverage, and your dedicated equipment order.

Recruitment Process FAQs

Key details about interviewing with us.

Is the take-home assessment paid?

Our standard assessment takes only 2 hours and uses simulated dummy data. For senior leadership roles requiring extensive strategic architecture presentations, we compensate candidates for their preparation time.

Can I reapply if I wasn't selected previously?

Yes. We encourage candidates to reapply after 6 months once they have accumulated further project experience or expanded their skill set.
