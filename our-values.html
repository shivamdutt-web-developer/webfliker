ETHICAL STANDARDS

The 11 Values We Live, Hire & Promote By

These are not slogans printed on office posters. They are operational standards referenced in every daily standup.

1. Client Success is Our Only North Star

We win only when our clients win. We evaluate our contribution by bottom-line commercial revenue and market share, never vanity impressions.

2. Absolute Algorithmic Integrity

We never deploy deceptive cloaking, automated link schemes, or private blog networks that jeopardize a client's long-term digital domain authority.

3. Unfiltered Radical Transparency

If a campaign test underperforms, we communicate immediately with diagnostic data and corrective action. We never bury disappointing figures in confusing reports.

4. Obsession with Craft & Precision

Good enough is not good enough. From 60fps WebGL shaders to pixel-perfect micro-interactions and clean code commits, we take immense pride in our craft.

5. Continuous Algorithmic Curiosity

Search and web technologies evolve rapidly. We continuously experiment with generative LLMs, knowledge graphs, and Core Web Vitals to stay ahead of Google updates.

6. Respect, Empathy & Squad Ownership

We foster a workplace free of toxic politics and micromanagement. Every squad owns their outcomes and supports one another across global time zones.

DAILY PRACTICE

How These Values Shape Our Daily Engineering

Concrete operational choices we make every day to uphold our standards.

No Vanity Metrics

We never report on irrelevant non-converting search volume just to show an upward graph. We focus strictly on qualified commercial intent.

Direct Director Access

Clients always communicate directly with technical directors and lead architects—never non-technical sales intermediaries.

Zero Subcontracting

Every single line of code, schema tag, and content deliverable is crafted in-house by our full-time 120+ person delivery team.
