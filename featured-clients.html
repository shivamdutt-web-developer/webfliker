Enterprise Client FAQs

Key details regarding our client onboarding, engagement models, and reporting standards.

What is Webflikr's minimum enterprise engagement size?

Our dedicated monthly search and experience retainers typically begin at $1,500/month or ₹75,000/month for comprehensive strategy, technical engineering, content production, and digital PR.

Do you work with direct brand competitors?

No. We enforce strict territorial and categorical exclusivity agreements. If we represent the leading brand in your specific category and geography, we will decline conflicting accounts to protect your competitive advantage.

How quickly does client onboarding occur?

Onboarding takes 5 business days. During this sprint, we complete technical access provisioning, server log pipeline integration, competitive benchmarking, and kick off our 100-point initial audit.

Who manages our account on a day-to-day basis?

Every enterprise client is assigned a Lead Search Director with minimum 7+ years of industry experience, supported by a Technical Architect, a Content Strategist, and a Data Analyst.
