Frequently Asked Questions

PPC Management Plans FAQs

Everything you need to know about billing, ad spends, and performance expectations.

Does your management fee include the actual ad spend?

No. Your advertising budget is paid directly from your credit card or billing account to Google, Meta, or Microsoft. Our management fee covers full campaign architecture, daily bidding optimization, ad copywriting, tracking, and weekly reporting.

Do we retain ownership of our ad accounts and data?

100% yes. We build and manage campaigns inside your own Google Ads and Meta Business Manager accounts. You own all historical campaign data, audience lists, and conversion pixels forever.

How quickly can we see results after launching?

PPC generates instant traffic on day one. During the first 14 days, smart bidding algorithms learn your highest-converting audience segments. By days 30 to 45, Quality Scores rise, average CPCs drop, and target ROAS stabilizes at scale.
