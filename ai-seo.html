Generative AI Search Questions & Answers

Understanding the transition from traditional Google algorithms to LLM generative citation.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing digital assets, entity relationships, and editorial citations so that generative AI models (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI) synthesize and reference your brand when users ask conversational questions.

Traditional search and AI conversational answers are merging. Google's AI Overviews draw from the same authoritative entity graphs and high-DA editorial mentions as standard algorithmic search. Brands that establish deep entity authority will dominate both channels.

We monitor prompt citation frequency (how often your brand is recommended for target queries), referral traffic from AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot), and branded organic search lift across Google.
