Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Webflikr ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. When you request an SEO audit, proposal, or contact our search directors, we may collect your name, business email, phone number, and website URL solely to provide technical search evaluations.

2. How We Use Client Data

Data gathered through technical diagnostics is strictly used to evaluate domain health, Core Web Vitals, organic search opportunities, and algorithmic compliance. We never sell, rent, or trade client information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use first-party analytics and performance cookies to understand visitor flow and optimize loading speeds across our 3D WebGL experiences. You may disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting core site usability.

4. Data Security & Non-Disclosure

All client communications, search audits, and technical architectures are protected under strict mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Data is transmitted via 256-bit SSL encryption and stored on SOC2-compliant enterprise cloud infrastructure.
