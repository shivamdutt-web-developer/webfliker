FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

E-Commerce Search Engineering FAQs

Answers to critical questions regarding catalog indexing, Shopify apps, and crawl budget governance.

E-commerce websites face unique architectural challenges that standard websites do not: thousands of dynamically generated faceted navigation URLs, pagination loops, out-of-stock product handling, duplicate product variant descriptions, and critical schema markup for price and availability. Our approach focuses heavily on technical catalog governance and high-intent transactional search terms.

For temporarily out-of-stock items, we keep the URL live, update schema availability to 'OutOfStock', and recommend related in-stock items to preserve page authority. For permanently discontinued items, we implement 301 redirects to the closest parent category or updated successor product to prevent 404 crawl budget decay.
