Frequently Asked Questions

Social Media Marketing Plans FAQs

Clear answers about approvals, reels production, and organic audience growth.

Do we get to approve posts and videos before they go live?

Yes, absolutely. We present a complete monthly content calendar with finalized visual designs, copy, and hashtags in advance. Nothing is published until your team formally signs off.

Do you buy followers or use automation bots?

Never. Buying bot followers destroys algorithmic reach and risks account shadowbans. All our audience growth is 100% organic through high-retention Reels, SEO keywords in captions, shareable carousels, and genuine community engagement.

How do you create reels if our team is located in another city?

We have two streamlined methods: 1) You capture simple smartphone video clips or product footage following our exact visual storyboards, and our motion editors transform them into cinema-grade Reels; or 2) We produce high-impact animated motion graphic and typography reels in-house.
