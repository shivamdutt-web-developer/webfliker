Frequently Asked Questions

Google Maps & Local SEO FAQs

Clear answers about timelines, ranking factors, and local citation protocols.

How long does it take to rank in Google's local 3-Pack?

For moderate-competition areas, initial ranking movements typically appear within 30 to 60 days following profile optimization and citation cleanup. In hyper-competitive metro hubs (like South Delhi or South Mumbai), full top-3 dominance typically matures within 90 to 120 days.

What is NAP consistency and why does Google care?

NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone number. When search engine bots crawl hundreds of directories and find matching, consistent details everywhere, Google gains 100% confidence in your physical location and rewards you with top 3-Pack placement.

Can you help my business if we have multiple locations?

Yes! Our Metro Dominance and Enterprise Chain packages are specifically engineered for multi-unit retail, dental chains, diagnostic labs, and franchises, providing centralized citation governance and individual locality landing pages.

Do reviews impact my Google Maps rankings?

Yes, review velocity, overall star ratings, and the presence of keywords in customer reviews are top-3 ranking factors in Google's local search algorithm.
